## Usage
- Primary horizontal mark = faceted symbol + two-line wordmark.
- Stacked mark; standalone symbol; square icon on midnight ground; dark/light; strict monochrome versions.
- Strong negative space, optical/forensic geometry. Avoid generic AI brains, gavels, shields, antique crests, extra ornaments and glossy gradients unrelated to the approved original.
- Maintain strong favicon legibility. Use actual original vector master and separately supplied logo files for production. This screenshot is a **brand-approval reference**, not an editable vector master. Do not claim to have vectorised/authenticated it.
- Responsive software layout stays headline-first and two-click action oriented; full sources remain collapsible. Keep status and confidence evidence-grounded.

## Brand claim guardrail
"Verified" describes the platform's evidence-validation process and aspiration. Do NOT visually label an individual object, attribution, source or price VERIFIED unless its actual trust gate has been satisfied. Do not import mock-dashboard examples in the brand concept as real inventory data. No generic maker/date or 92% illustrated confidence may appear as facts about a user's item.
