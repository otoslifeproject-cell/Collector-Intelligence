# Selling source checks — 23 October 2026

These are current platform research notes, not item valuations, account eligibility
approval, packing quotations or listing publication. No production data was changed.
Every actual route still needs the owner's seller status, selected category,
destination, packing measurements, delivery/insurance quote and fee date.

- **eBay UK private:** the UK-private section, updated30September2026, states no
  final-value/regulatory seller fee for eligible ordinary UK sales, with300 free
  monthly listings. Optional upgrades, excess listings and overseas delivery can
  cost extra. The same page has a separate non-UK section with different rates;
  do not mistake that section for the UK-private rule. Source:
  https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/fees-private-sellers-activated-managed-payments?id=4822
- **eBay buyer total:** buyer protection added to eligible private listings is
  separate from seller proceeds. Offers/auction prices shown to buyers can include
  it, while the seller sees a smaller receipt. Do not use an ended listing's
  buyer-facing price as confirmed seller gross or infer that it sold. Source:
  https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/selling/pricing-items/buyer-protection-information-sellers?id=5595
- **eBay UK business:** Pottery,Ceramics&Glass category870 currently lists10.9%
  final-value fee plus an order fee (normally40p above£10), beforeVAT. Fee basis
  includes postage/taxes; additional charges can apply. This is not an all-in rate
  and cannot be substituted for actual account/category conditions. Source:
  https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/fees-business-sellers?id=4809
- **Etsy UK:** its UK selling page lists6.5% transaction plus4%+20p payment
  processing, listing charges and possible offsite-ad charges. Extra applicable
  fees/taxes must be checked before calculating net; this is not a universal
  all-in10.5% fee. Source: https://www.etsy.com/uk/sell . Vintage eligibility needs
  an evidenced age of at least20years; appearance alone is insufficient:
  https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity/ . A regulatory-fee help page could not be
  accessed during this check, so no exact current regulatory rate is asserted.
- **Vinted UK:** current UK catalogue rules distinguish non-Pro and Pro policies.
  Home/collectables availability does not establish this account's business or
  fragile-item shipping eligibility. Use the actual UK rules, category and carrier
  cover before recommending it: https://www.vinted.co.uk/catalog-rules . No exact
  Vinted seller fee or breakage insurance claim is verified in these notes.

No paid promotions, subscriptions or paid research requests were started. Asking
prices and illustrative platform arithmetic do not establish achieved value.
Auction/dealer routes need actual written commission, reserve, transport and
unsold/withdrawal terms; these are not assumed free. Selling times remain explicit
scenario assumptions rather than guarantees. Preserve official-source checks with
the final object assessment and confirm them again before publication.
