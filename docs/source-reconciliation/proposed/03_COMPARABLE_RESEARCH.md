# SKILL — COMPARABLE RESEARCH
Version: 4.0-aligned-draft-20261020
Status: REVIEW CANDIDATE — not published or approved

## Authority and purpose
Build a defensible comparable set under Master Brief v4.0 and Source & Evidence Policy v4.0. If this workflow conflicts with either, follow the controlling document and record the conflict. Preserve the original workflow and rejected comparables.

## Retrieve before searching
Inspect object-specific photographs and owner observations, then retrieve internal records with reproducible references. Check verification and freshness. Search externally only for material gaps; never transfer a mark, measurement or comparable between objects by assumption.

## Source priority
Apply the v4.0 policy's price hierarchy to the claim, not to a website name alone:

### Tier A
- directly verified specialist/recognised auction realised results;
- directly verified marketplace sold results;
- maker/factory/museum/catalogue documentation for attribution.

### Tier B
- recognised auction aggregators/databases;
- eBay SOLD/COMPLETED;
- other genuine completed-sale sources.

### Tier C
- dealer sold archive where achieved price is confirmed;
- dealer archive where only former asking price is known;
- auction estimates.

### Tier D
- current dealer asking;
- 1stDibs asking;
- Etsy asking;
- Vinted asking;
- eBay active asking;
- other active marketplace asks.

Tier D is context, not valuation evidence. Museum documentation can support attribution without supplying a resale price. A dealer archive does not establish achieved consideration unless that price is confirmed. A completed listing or indexed result alone does not establish a directly verified sale.

## Search method
1. Exact maker + pattern/design + object type.
2. Maker + object type + colour/size.
3. Maker + closely related form.
4. Region/period + highly similar form only if maker remains uncertain.

Do not start by collecting vague visual lookalikes. Keep maker attribution separate from market attribution.

## Comparable record and verification
Retain all mandatory comparable fields, price types and verification statuses from the v4.0 policy. Preserve the exact URL/reference, lot/item ID, check date, sale/listing date, currency, raw price, premium status, size, condition, colour, attribution and similarity notes. Record converted prices separately.

Use VERIFIED_DIRECT only after independently checking that the exact retained source supports the claim. A model-generated label is not verification. Preserve original claims, contradictions, failed checks and rejected hypotheses.

## Similarity score
- 30 maker/pattern
- 15 object type
- 10 size
- 10 colour
- 10 age
- 10 condition
- 5 signed/labelled
- 5 venue quality
- 5 recency

Apply judgement after scoring. A matching score does not authenticate attribution or a sale. Reject/heavily discount different makers, unrelated forms, material size/condition mismatches, seller speculation, isolated outliers, asking-only evidence and unverifiable sold claims as primary valuation support.

## Output and write-back
Keep hammer, premium-inclusive realised, marketplace sold, confirmed dealer sold, former ask/achieved unknown, auction estimate, dealer ask, marketplace ask and indexed/unverified prices distinct. Never average them as equivalent evidence.

Keep identification, dating and valuation confidence separate. Label insufficient sold evidence LOW or VERY LOW / PROVISIONAL; keep valuation and buyer routing conditional on uncertain attribution. End with the v4.0 research-stop decision and best next action.

Write reusable verified findings with references and uncertainty preserved. Give time-sensitive evidence last_verified and freshness requirements. Keep unsupported guesses and ambiguous price claims explicitly unverified, without promoting them to durable facts.
