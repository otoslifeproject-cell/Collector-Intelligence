## Stage 2 — Lot Optimiser

- Preserve physical items and their research. Proposed groups reference exact
  owner/item/photo IDs, relationship evidence and uncertainty; colour or UV glow
  alone does not establish an original matched set.
- Retain comparable lots as indivisible totals with quantity, composition,
  condition, maker confidence, venue/date/currency and premium basis. Individual
  prices remain unknown without independently verified decomposition.
- Compare all solo, matching sets, curated bundles, hybrid and auction/dealer
  clearance plans under FAST CASH/BALANCED/MAX VALUE. Include complete fees/VAT,
  minimum/unsold charges, packing, insured delivery, returns/breakage, probability,
  time and workload. Distinguish net proceeds, expected proceeds and profit.
- Allow overlapping hypothetical drafts. Atomic approved sale reservations must
  stop the same physical item appearing in two approved listings/consignments,
  including individual listings. Cancel/release/split preserves revisions, photos
  and history. Whole-lot outcomes never invent component sale prices.
- Add object-level grouping opportunities and collection-level comparison cards,
  with explicit assumptions, evidence gaps and reversible owner approval.
- Regressions include A&G £320/three pieces, Fieldings £38/four pieces,
  Davidson £120/pair, Potters £30/seven-piece set and £40/five bowls. They prove
  neither a unit price nor an automatic bundle premium.
