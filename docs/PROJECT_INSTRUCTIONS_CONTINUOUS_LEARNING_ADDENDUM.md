This supplements existing Project Instructions and skills. It never supersedes 02_MASTER_PROJECT_BRIEF.md or 03_SOURCE_AND_EVIDENCE_POLICY.md.

## Non-negotiable mission
Every photograph, item, research session, correction, external reference, price observation, buyer encounter and actual sale must improve future Collector Intelligence decisions through retained, auditable records. Do not treat a conversation, model answer or document archive as equivalent to a populated Knowledge Brain. Do not claim continuous learning until write-back and retrieval are verified.

## Every investigation
1. Preserve original inputs: source conversation/document reference, images and indices, object separation, marks, measurements, original notes, date, provenance and object identity. Retain full original transcript/export separately.
2. Retrieve internal-first: search prior object/evidence/attribution versions, source claims, marks, confidence failures, comparable records and sale outcomes; log retrieved record IDs and freshness. Never invent a past result or silently restart research.
3. Grade separately: photographic observation, identification attribution (0–100), dating confidence (0–100), valuation confidence (0–100), authority of design/maker reference, strength of match to this object and evidence for a realised sale. Record contradictions and plausible alternatives.
4. Research with source audit: named source, producer, exact URL or reference, date inspected, original wording/lot, sale date, price definition, currency/premium, size, condition, signature/label, match score and verification method. A respected museum establishes design history, not the authenticity or price of an unrelated bowl.
5. Append knowledge: write new object-level observations, rival hypotheses, original sources, reason for revision, confidence changes, price distinctions, listing decision and eventual sale-outcome feedback. A model-generated VERIFIED_DIRECT status is only a claim until checked.
6. Learn from errors: save false-positive and false-negative examples, ambiguous inscription/scratch/lighting, wrong image grouping, outlier/asking-price inflation and user corrections as regression fixtures. Re-use them in later analysis.
7. Safeguard commercial results: show auction hammer/private/retail/quick sale/net separately, fees, time-to-sell and realistic evidence-weighted ranges. Never build pricing confidence from asking-price comparisons alone.
8. Close the loop: log what was saved, what was not, source hashes, active revisions, next investigation, build/deploy/test status and costs. If tools are unavailable, produce an export package; do not claim database or Drive success.
9. Master PDF imported as a historical transcript source, not independently verified object or auction evidence; its earlier appraisal revisions must remain distinct chronological versions. Other Project windows are NOT implicitly included in it.
10. Owner security and permanence: correct project bwdafrwkimjvwfoqomot only; owner RLS, append-only source history, private photos, no credentials in exports, no unapproved permanent item IDs, no deletion of originals.
